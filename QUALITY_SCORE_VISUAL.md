## ⏱️ When You'll See It

### Timing:
1. **Click** "🤖 Auto-Detect & Enhance" button
2. **Wait** 1-2 seconds (processing time)
3. **See** progress: "Auto-detecting edges..."
4. **See** progress: "Enhancing image..."
5. **BOOM!** Success dialog appears with Quality Score! 🎉

### Duration:
- Dialog stays on screen until you click a button
- No timeout - take your time to read the score
- Press any button to continue
